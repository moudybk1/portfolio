Footer layout refinement — 2026-10-15

Design read: a calm, professional closing chapter for potential collaborators,
using the portfolio's existing DM Sans / Geist Mono, neutral black and warm
cream palette, and restrained motion. DESIGN_VARIANCE 6 / MOTION_INTENSITY 6 /
VISUAL_DENSITY 3. Original wordmark, copy, destinations and page count retained.

Audit at 1280×720: the old composition used a 64px wordmark and three separate
columns with 14px links. The brand took a wide column while navigation and
social links appeared small and disconnected. Footer height was 311.5px inside
the 643px chapter stage.

New composition:
- Identity and two-column navigation share the upper row on desktop.
- Four social profiles form a full-width lower row, with consistent icon space,
  visible external-link arrows and thin dividers.
- Mobile puts identity above two compact navigation/profile columns. Every
  portrait link has a 44px click target; compact landscape uses smaller rows.
- Copyright sits below the link groups with a quiet, consistent type scale.
- The same component and proportions are used on Articles and article pages.
- Footer.module.css owns its visual styles so previous global footer rules do
  not change the component when navigating between routes.
- Chapter entrance motion uses stable data attributes, independently of CSS
  module class names. External-arrow hover feedback respects reduced motion.
